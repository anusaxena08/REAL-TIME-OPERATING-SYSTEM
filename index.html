🔒 Priority Inversion & Inheritance Laboratory

Sha, Rajkumar & Lehoczky (1990) Priority Inheritance Protocol demonstration

The Phenomenon: Logger (Priority 1) locks the shared buffer at t=0. Sensor (Priority 3, urgent) arrives at t=1 and blocks. Control (Priority 2, medium) arrives at t=2 and preempts Logger because it needs no lock. Without inheritance, Control indirectly delays Sensor for 4 ticks (Priority Inversion). With inheritance, Logger inherits Priority 3, finishes its critical section promptly, and unblocks Sensor at t=3!
